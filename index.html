How it works

From post to riding crew.

1

Post your trip

Add source, destination, dates, vehicle, pace, and your riding habits and preferences.

2

Receive requests

Other riders going the same direction send requests. You see their profile, bike, habits, and proposed meet-up point before deciding.

3

Accept and chat

Accept the people who fit. A shared chat opens immediately to coordinate logistics.

4

Ride together

Meet at the agreed point. Share the route, the dhabas, the passes, and the memories.

Safety first

Ride together safely.

These rules are visible before anyone connects or confirms plans.

Verify before meeting

ID verification, profile photos, linked socials, and experience history before any contact.

First meet in public

Suggest a dhaba, petrol station, or popular landmark as the first meeting point.

Share your route

Share your live itinerary with a trusted contact before leaving. Update it as plans change.

Discuss pace upfront

Agree on daily distances, stop schedules, and what to do if someone falls behind before the trip starts.

Emergency contacts

Every profile should have an emergency contact visible to matched riding partners.

Clear exit rules

Anyone can leave the group ride at any time. No pressure. Solo continuation is always valid.
