Safety guidelines

Travel together with clear boundaries.

Matching is only useful if people feel safe. These rules should be visible before travelers meet or confirm plans.

Verify first

Encourage ID verification, profile photos, linked socials, and completed travel preferences before messaging.

Meet publicly

Suggest first meetups in cafes, hotel lobbies, stations, or popular public areas before private plans.

Share plans

Let users save emergency contacts, share itinerary details, and keep arrival times visible to trusted people.

Control comfort filters

Support women-only matching, verified-only matching, group-size limits, and no-nightlife preferences.

Report and block

Every profile, chat, and trip group should include clear report, block, and remove-from-trip actions.

Confirm before booking

Travelers should agree on budgets, accommodation, transport, cancellation rules, and personal boundaries first.
